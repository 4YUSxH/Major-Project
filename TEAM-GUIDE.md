# 🎓 Sathyabama Help Desk - Team Operations & Testing Guide

This document is specifically written for the Major Project team members. Our application relies heavily on dynamic Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). The interface completely changes based on the type of user who is currently authenticated.

This guide will explain exactly how to test both sides of the application.

---

## 🔐 1. Authentication & Role Pathways

Our system uses a single unified `/login` portal that intelligently routes privileges based on your role. However, creating the initial accounts requires navigating to different signup pages.

### 👤 Testing as a Student
1. **Create Account:** Navigate to [`http://localhost:5173/register`](http://localhost:5173/register) (or click "Sign Up" on the landing page). 
2. **Details:** Enter a generic student name and email.
3. **Login:** Navigating to [`http://localhost:5173/login`](http://localhost:5173/login) lets you sign in standardly.

### 🛡️ Testing as Staff / Admin
To prevent students from creating staff accounts, the Staff Registration route is deliberately hidden from the main navigation.
1. **Create Account:** You must manually type [`http://localhost:5173/register-staff`](http://localhost:5173/register-staff) into your URL bar.
2. **Role Selection:** Fill out your staff profile and explicitly assign yourself the **Staff/Faculty** or **System Admin** role in the dropdown menu.
3. **Login:** After creation, you use the exact same standard login page ([`http://localhost:5173/login`](http://localhost:5173/login)). The backend recognizes your staff email and boots up the authorized interface.

## ⚡ 2. Features Overview

Depending on which account you log in with above, the system responds entirely differently. Keep this in mind during your major project presentation.

### 📉 Student View (Standard User)
When you log in as a Student, you are in a "Read & Request" mode.
* **Dashboard Analytics:** Shows personal stats (e.g. "My Total Tickets").
* **Ticketing System:** 
  * Can *Create* new tickets prioritizing their queries.
  * Can only *View* their own tickets. Cannot see tickets generated by other students.
* **Notice Board:** Can read campus-wide public announcements and view attached circulars/images.
* **Knowledge Base:** Can read IT and campus FAQs in the interactive expanding cards.

### 📈 Staff / Admin View (Elevated User)
When you log in as Staff, the platform transforms into a "Command Center" prioritizing resolution and outbound communication.
* **Dashboard Analytics:** Aggregates metrics for the *entire* campus (showing total system loads, etc).
* **Ticketing System (Resolution Mode):**
  * Views a master list of **ALL** student tickets ever created.
  * Can open any ticket, change its status (Open ➡️ In Progress ➡️ Resolved), and drop official resolution replies.
* **Notice Board (Broadcast Mode):** 
  * Gains the massive "Post Notice" button. 
  * Can attach files/images natively to broadcast updates campus-wide. 
  * *Security Feature:* Staff can delete notices, but strictly **only** the ones they specifically authored.
* **Knowledge Base:** Gains the ability to author new static IT troubleshooting policies and delete legacy ones via the Trash icon.
